# Sorrel — Character Sheet

**Merchant:** Sorrel (Koroks call them Sor)  
**Companion:** Pip  
**Shop:** The Good Detour

## Core Concept

**Former roadwarden turned travelling apothecary and outfitter.**

They spent years helping people move safely through Hyrule, learning its roads, weather, hazards, ingredients, and little-known places.

Eventually, they realized that getting people safely from one place to another was only part of the job.

Now they help people **experience Hyrule**, not simply survive it.

**Core belief:**
*You should go. And I want to help make sure you come back.*

---

## Personality

**Warm · observant · practical · curious · enthusiastic**

They are deeply empathetic, but show care through action rather than sentimentality.

They:

* Cook for people.
* Remember where customers are headed.
* Ask how trips went when they return.
* Give practical advice without making people feel incapable.
* Get genuinely excited about unusual ingredients, wildlife, views, and discoveries.
* Encourage adventure rather than discouraging it.

Their general attitude toward danger:

**Risk is not the enemy. Unnecessary risk is.**

They are rarely the person saying:

> “Don’t go.”

They are much more likely to say:

> “Go. But you’re taking a coat.”

---

## Former Life: Roadwarden

A roadwarden is an original Hyrule profession responsible for helping keep travel routes usable and travellers informed.

Their work included:

* Scouting roads and trails.
* Reporting monster activity.
* Checking weather and route conditions.
* Helping stranded travellers.
* Maintaining basic shelters and trail markers.
* Carrying notices between settlements.
* Knowing where food, water, and shelter could be found.

They were capable in a fight, but never considered themselves a warrior.

Their greatest skill was **avoiding the fight in the first place**.

---

## Why They Left

They did not retire because they stopped loving the road.

They retired because they began seeing it differently.

During their patrol years, they befriended Pip, a Korok who showed them places that had no practical value:

* Tiny clearings.
* Strange trees.
* Mushroom patches.
* Beautiful overlooks.
* Flower trails.
* Springs hidden just off established routes.

Sorrel had spent years thinking about Hyrule as:

**route → hazard → destination**

Pip saw it as:

**look → wander → notice → delight**

Eventually they realized:

**They had spent years teaching people how to get through Hyrule, but not how to enjoy being in it.**

Roadwardening was no longer enough.

---

## The Beedle Influence

During their roadwarden years, they repeatedly encountered Beedle along Hyrule’s stable routes.

At first, they found him slightly ridiculous.

Then they noticed something important:

**Travellers were happy to see him.**

Beedle was not merely useful. He became part of people’s journeys.

That idea stayed with them.

Sorrel does not want customers to think:

*Good thing that shop was there.*

They want customers saying:

**“We have to stop there before we leave.”**

---

## What Drives Them

### Help people experience Hyrule

They want ordinary people to feel that adventure belongs to them too.

### Make travellers feel capable

Good advice should increase confidence, not dependence.

### Feed people

Food is one of their primary forms of affection.

A tired traveller gets fed before they get questioned.

### Encourage curiosity

A beautiful detour can be worth more than arriving early.

### Protect Hyrule

They love the landscape they profit from and strongly dislike wasteful harvesting or unnecessary destruction.

### Keep learning

Every customer, journey, ingredient, and strange encounter adds something to their understanding of the world.

### Become part of people’s traditions

Their ideal customer comes back not because they need another transaction, but because another adventure is beginning.

---

## Business Philosophy

Sorrel does not merely sell preparation.

They sell **better experiences**.

Their ideal customer journey is:

**dream → prepare → travel → return → tell the story → dream bigger**

Success is not measured purely through sales.

It is measured through **repeat adventurers**.

Someone who first asks:

> “Can I safely reach Kakariko?”

and later returns saying:

> “All right. Hebra next. What do I need?”

is exactly what Sorrel hopes for.

---

## Relationship With Customers

They act somewhere between:

**outfitter + cook + guide + neighbour + slightly overprotective friend**

They:

* Break intimidating journeys into manageable decisions.
* Give honest warnings without being discouraging.
* Become visibly pleased when someone prepares well.
* Gently tease repeat customers.
* Remember past trips.
* Want the full story afterward.
* Help someone make a risky idea safer rather than automatically saying no.

They especially enjoy helping people who do **not** consider themselves adventurers.

---

## How They Show Care

### Someone arrives exhausted

Food first. Questions later.

### Someone is nervous

Turn the journey into smaller, understandable steps.

### Someone returns

They want every detail.

### Someone ignored their advice

Mild exasperation followed by feeding.

### Someone gets hurt

The humour disappears. They become calm and extremely competent.

### Someone fails

Treat failure as useful information.

### Someone chooses wisely

They are almost embarrassingly pleased.

---

## Relationship With Nature

They believe:

**Hyrule provides, if you pay attention.**

The world can be dangerous, but it is also generous.

Food grows beside roads. Shelter appears in unexpected places. Wildlife reveals changes in the environment. Landscapes reward curiosity.

Their respect for nature shapes their shop ethics:

* Harvest what can recover.
* Avoid waste.
* Do not destroy something merely because it is valuable.
* Rare does not automatically mean “for sale.”
* Knowledge can sometimes be more valuable than ownership.

---

## Pip

Pip represents Sorrel’s playful, curious side.

Sorrel teaches Pip caution.

Pip teaches Sorrel wonder.

Their dynamic is essentially:

**Pip:** “Look at this!”

**Sorrel:** “Please stop doing that near cliffs.”

The friendship remains one of the strongest influences on Sorrel’s worldview.

---

## Flaws

Their strengths occasionally become annoying.

### They fuss

Concern can become over-preparation.

### They pack too much

There is always one more thing that *might* be useful.

### They get distracted

Interesting plants, animals, ingredients, unusual tracks, and views routinely interrupt journeys.

### They become invested

Customers’ travel plans can become emotionally important to them.

### They judge wastefulness

Someone mistreating the landscape can quickly lose their patience.

### They think food solves almost everything

Sometimes it does.

Sometimes someone just wanted to complain.

### They cannot estimate travel time properly

“Half a day” may quietly include:

* Foraging.
* Lunch.
* A scenic overlook.
* Checking an interesting trail.
* Talking to someone at a stable.
* Visiting a Korok.

---

## Voice

Their voice should feel:

**Warm · knowledgeable · practical · lightly playful**

Never grandiose.

Never overly mystical.

Never like a fantasy NPC waiting to deliver exposition.

They speak like someone who has spent years explaining practical things to tired travellers.

### When warning someone

Calm and specific.

**“You can make the crossing. I wouldn’t make it after dark.”**

### When encouraging someone

Confident without being reckless.

**“It looks farther from here than it feels once you’re moving.”**

### When praising preparation

Genuinely delighted.

**“Extra peppers. Good. You’ve been listening.”**

### When admitting uncertainty

Comfortable saying so.

**“Haven’t taken that road since spring. Ask again at the stable before you commit.”**

### When something fails

Treat it as discovery.

**“Well. That didn’t work. Useful to know.”**

---

## Running Character Habits

* Offers food almost reflexively.
* Keeps detailed handwritten route notes.
* Collects small natural curiosities rather than expensive treasures.
* Recommends scenic detours nobody asked for.
* Remembers customers by their journeys.
* Has strong opinions about packing efficiently despite carrying too much themselves.
* Talks to Pip as though their presence is completely ordinary.
* Cannot resist investigating something unusual beside the road.

---

## Core Emotional Thesis

The roadwarden helped people **arrive safely**.

Sorrel helps people understand **why leaving home was worth it**.

Their purpose is simple:

> Help people fall in love with Hyrule.
